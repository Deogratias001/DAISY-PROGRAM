# Programme Overview

## The need

Adolescent girls in Mombasa's underserved communities grow up without accurate, age-appropriate information about their bodies and sexual health. Puberty, menstruation, consent and contraception remain taboo at home, teachers lack training and teaching aids, and stigma keeps girls away from youth-friendly health services. The result: myths, missed school days, early and unintended pregnancies, STIs, and girls who cannot recognise or report abuse.

## What DAISY does

DAISY gives girls informed, safe and accessible SRHR education through:

1. **Safe-space sessions** — small groups of 25 girls, led by trained facilitators from trusted community organisations.
2. **Hands-on 3D-printed models** — reproductive anatomy, the menstrual cycle, pregnancy and contraception, printed by Eco-Prints Generation from recycled coastal plastic.
3. **Linkage to services** — facilitators point girls to youth-friendly health and protection services.

## Delivery model

- A **session** serves 50 girls in one day, split into a **morning** and an **evening sub-session** of 25 girls each.
- Each sub-session has **one facilitator**.
- Each girl attends **2 sessions** (Session 1 and Session 2), always in the same group.
- Every sub-session is reported on the Eco-Prints template within 24 hours.
- Eco-Prints Generation provides media coverage, under the rules in `safeguarding-and-media.md`.
