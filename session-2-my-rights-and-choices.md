# Session 2 — My Rights and Choices

**Duration:** 3 hours · **Group:** the same 25 girls as Session 1 · **Facilitator:** the same facilitator where possible
**Materials:** 3D-printed pregnancy and contraception models, adolescent guide books, scenario cards, local referral list, anonymous question box

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the session, girls can:

1. Explain their sexual and reproductive health rights.
2. Describe consent and the signs of healthy and unhealthy relationships.
3. Recognise gender-based violence and know where to report it.
4. Name youth-friendly health and protection services near them.
5. Set one personal goal for their health and education.
