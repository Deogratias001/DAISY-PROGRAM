# Safeguarding, Consent and Media Rules

All DAISY participants are minors. These rules apply to every partner, facilitator, volunteer and media team member.

## Consent

- A signed **guardian consent form** is required before a girl joins a session.
- A **separate media consent** (guardian signature plus the girl's own agreement) is required before she is photographed, filmed or interviewed.
- Signed forms are stored on the partners' secure drive — **never in this repository**.

## Safeguarding

- Facilitators never meet a girl alone in a closed room.
- Any disclosure of abuse, violence or risk is reported to the partner's safeguarding lead **the same day**, then referred to the right service.
- Reports describe concerns **without names**.
- [Add safeguarding lead names and contacts for each partner]

## Media coverage (led by Eco-Prints Generation)

**Do**
- Photograph and film activities, the 3D models, facilitators and girls with media consent.
- Record documentary-style interviews only with consenting girls, facilitators and partner leaders.
- Credit UNFPA, YSW and the delivery partner in published content.

**Don't**
- Show any girl without media consent — seat her outside the frame.
- Publish a girl's full name, school, home location or any identifying detail.
- Record personal questions, disclosures or sensitive moments.
- Post content showing a girl in a way that could embarrass or stigmatise her.

## Data protection

- Personal data stays in the partners' secure drive with restricted access.
- Reports shared in this repository are anonymised and aggregated.
- Delete raw media of girls who later withdraw consent.
