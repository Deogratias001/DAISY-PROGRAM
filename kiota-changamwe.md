# Work Plan — KIOTA CBO, Changamwe Sub-county

KIOTA CBO delivers DAISY SRHR sessions to **250 girls across all five wards of Changamwe sub-county in two weeks**. Eco-Prints Generation coordinates delivery, supplies the reporting template and leads media coverage.

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Implementing partner | KIOTA CBO (session delivery) |
| Coordinating partner | Eco-Prints Generation (coordination, reporting, media) |
| Target | 250 girls |
| Duration | 2 weeks (10 working days) — start date TBC |
| Delivery volume | 10 sessions = 20 sub-sessions of 25 girls |

Each girl is registered to one fixed group (morning or evening) and stays with the same peers and facilitator for both sessions. Register 5 reserves per ward to protect the target.

## Roles

| Party | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| KIOTA CBO | Mobilise and register girls; book venues; assign facilitators; collect consent forms; deliver sessions; submit daily and weekly reports |
| Facilitators | Lead one sub-session; keep the attendance register; submit the sub-session report within 24 hours; flag safeguarding concerns the same day |
| Eco-Prints Generation | Coordination and UNFPA liaison; reporting template and review; SRHR models and refreshments as agreed; media coverage; consolidated report |
